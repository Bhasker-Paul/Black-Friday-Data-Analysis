# Black-Friday-Data-Analysis
Black Friday Sales Data Analysis
Project Overview

This project analyzes Black Friday retail sales data using Python to understand purchasing behavior and identify key factors influencing customer spending.

Through data cleaning, preprocessing, and visualization, the analysis explores how variables such as product category, gender, age group, occupation, and city category affect purchase amounts.

The results suggest that product category plays a more significant role in determining spending patterns than demographic factors.

Objectives

Analyze customer purchasing behavior during Black Friday sales.

Identify factors influencing purchase amounts.

Explore relationships between demographic variables and spending.

Determine whether product category or demographics drive sales more strongly.

Dataset Information

The dataset contains customer purchase records collected during a Black Friday sale event.

Typical features include:

Feature	Description
User_ID	Unique customer ID
Product_ID	Unique product ID
Gender	Customer gender
Age	Age group of the customer
Occupation	Occupation category
City_Category	City classification
Stay_In_Current_City_Years	Years in current city
Marital_Status	Marital status
Product_Category	Category of purchased product
Purchase	Purchase amount
